## 2. Shift Reconciliation & Blind Till Balancing Rules

- **Opening Shift**: Cashier logs opening float (e.g. ₦15,000) before processing sales.
- **Blind Till Cash Count**: Cashier counts physical cash in drawer without seeing expected system cash total during closing.
- **Overage / Shortage Calculation**:
  - `Expected Cash = Opening Float + Cash Payments + Cash In - Cash Out (Safe Drops/Paid Out)`
  - `Variance = Counted Cash - Expected Cash`
  - Variances > ₦1,000 trigger automatic manager alert and require written explanation notes.
- **Shift Report Isolation**: Shift Z-Report isolates ONLY checks opened/closed by that specific cashier during their active shift window.
