## 4. POS Inventory Depletion & Void/Refund Reversal Architecture

1. **Automatic BOM Depletion on POS Checkout**:
   - When a combo or package is sold via `processAuthoritativePosPayment`:
     - Food components (Jollof Rice, Fried Rice, Chicken, Samosa, Puff Puff, etc.) are depleted based on their respective portion recipes.
     - The packaging container (`packaging.productId`) is automatically expanded into the inventory target list and depleted by 1 container unit under `transactionType: 'POS_DEPLETION'`.
2. **Atomic Inventory Restoration on Void/Refund**:
   - `createOrderInventoryReverser` executes full audit trail reversals for all depleted inventory lines.
   - Restores both food items and packaging container items back into active stock with full transactional metadata.
3. **Pricing Engine Separation**:
   - `calculateAuthoritativeItemAndOrderPricing` treats `packaging` as internal production BOM metadata.
   - The customer is charged strictly the package selling price (e.g., Chicken Combo is ₦5,000 flat; Beef Combo is ₦4,000 flat). No extra packaging surcharge is added to the customer check.
